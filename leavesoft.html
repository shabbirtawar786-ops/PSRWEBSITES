LevSoft

“Simplify Leave Tracking. Organized, Accurate, Effortless.”

Leave Management System, better known as LevSoft, is a complete solution for managing staff and faculty leave records with ease and accuracy. Developed for educational institutions and organizations of all sizes, LevSoft enables seamless tracking of leave applications, balances, and approvals—ensuring transparency, compliance, and total data security on a single, intuitive platform.

See Features

Core Features

Smart Leave Tracking:

Maintain detailed leave records for all faculty and staff members on a calendar year basis, with support for both standard and user-defined leave types (e.g., casual, earned, medical, or custom leaves).

Minimum Input – Maximum Output:

With minimal manual entry, LevSoft generates comprehensive outputs such as individual ledgers, monthly leave statements, and yearly leave summaries—instantly.

Advanced Reporting & Export Options:

Generate detailed reports and export them into multiple formats for easy sharing, recordkeeping, or audit purposes—covering ledgers, monthly summaries, yearly data, and more.

Robust Data Security:

Inbuilt protection ensures all leave data remains confidential and accessible only to authorized users—maintaining privacy and accuracy at every stage.

Shortcut Keys for Speed:

Integrated keyboard shortcuts enable fast, hassle-free data entry, navigation, and report generation, making LevSoft extremely user-friendly and efficient.

“Power Up Your Workforce with LevSoft”

LevSoft brings precision, control, and simplicity to leave management—empowering organizations with clarity, accountability, and effortless recordkeeping. It’s not just a leave tracker—it’s your institution’s HR partner in efficiency and discipline.
